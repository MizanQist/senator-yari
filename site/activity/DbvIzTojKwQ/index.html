Projects & giving7 August 2026Posted with @everythingyari

Thousands of people.

Hundreds of vehicles. One bridge. Every single day. An estimated 2,500 rely on the Bardoki Bridge daily, keeping people, goods, and services moving across the community. That translates to well over 800,000 people every year. The Bardoki bridge built by Senator Yari reduces travel time, lowers transport costs, improves access to markets, schools and healthcare, and supporting local economic activity. Infrastructure is not just about what is built. It is about the opportunities it creates and the economic value for the people that it serves. #TheYariFiles #InfrastructureForGrowth #Zamfara
