Senate

Zamfara West in the 10th Senate

Elected to the 10th Senate for Zamfara West in 2023 and named Chairman of the Senate Committee on Water Resources.

  • 18 September 2023

    Clean water for 133 million

    Setting out the Water Resources committee's agenda: legislate on the water challenges that leave 133 million Nigerians without clean water.

  • 26 September 2023

    Confirming the CBN leadership

    The Senate confirms Dr Yemi Cardoso as Governor of the Central Bank with four deputies; at the screening he presses on Forex access for small businesses.

  • 12 October 2023

    P20 Summit, New Delhi

    With the Speaker and Deputy Senate President, engaging G20 parliaments on the SDGs, digital literacy and sustainable energy.

  • 20 October 2023

    Fiscal policy retreat

    The Senate retreat on fiscal policy and tax reform in Ikot Ekpene, Akwa Ibom.

  • 26 October 2023

    Water Resources committee inaugurated

    Presiding over the first meeting: clean drinking water, sanitation, and water for energy, irrigation and transport.

  • 28 November 2023

    Police Trust Fund

    The Senate approves the Nigeria Police Trust Fund budget so officers are resourced to protect the country.

  • 29 November 2023

    Budget of Renewed Hope

    President Tinubu presents the 2024 Appropriation Bill to the National Assembly.

  • 7 December 2023

    Correctional Service budget defence

    The Joint Committee on Interior presses for transparency and humane treatment of inmates.

  • 12 December 2023

    Water and sanitation hearings

    First 2024 budget defence hearing of the Joint Committee on Water Resources and Sanitation.

  • 13 February 2024

    Security session

    A closed-door session with the National Security Adviser, service chiefs, ministers of Defence and Interior, the Inspector-General and the DSS.
