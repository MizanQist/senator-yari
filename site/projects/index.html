Projects

Projects and giving in Zamfara

Food, relief, health, education and infrastructure, funded personally and through the constituency office. Each figure comes from the Senator's own announcements.

Food security

#YariFeeds250k

Rice, grains, beans, maize and sugar to 250,000 households in every one of Zamfara's 147 wards during Ramadan 2024, regardless of political affiliation.

Flagged off in Talata Mafara on 11 March 2024 with a distribution committee chaired by Hon. Liman. The stories of the families it reached were told in the documentary Hope Amidst Hunger, broadcast on Arise Television on 12 May 2024.

  • 1.25 million people
  • 358 trucks
  • 147 ward centres
  • All 14 local governments
Disaster relief

Gummi flood recovery

After the 2024 floods took more than 2,000 homes in Gummi, fifteen trucks of relief supplies arrived first, then blocks, cement, builders and roofing until families had homes again.

Support came step by step: mattresses and blankets, then building materials and money to pay builders, then roofing to finish each house. The rebuilding committee was directed to use modern, durable materials so the tragedy would not repeat.

  • 15 trucks of relief supplies
  • 2,000+ homes lost, rebuilt in stages
  • Committee set up with the Emir of Gummi
