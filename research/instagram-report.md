## 4. Projects, donations and philanthropy

### Food distribution
- **#YariFeeds250k — Ramadan 2024** (flag-off 11 Mar 2024, Talata Mafara; C4Ypk0xstxz, C4XHQZsIgO_, C4WNvUxs_f7). Rice, grains, beans, maize and sugar to **250,000 households ≈ 1,250,000 people**, **358 trucks**, **147 ward distribution centres**, all **14 LGAs**, "regardless of political affiliation". Distribution committee chaired by Hon. Liman. Documentary **"Hope Amidst Hunger"** aired on Arise TV, Sun 12 May 2024, 6:30 pm (C629DLWMa9X).
- **Eid-el-Maulud 2023 distribution** (27 Sep 2023; CxsH7j_Mhkz): rice, maize and cows to all 14 LGAs / 147 wards; beneficiaries heads of households, widows, vulnerable groups; committees told to keep distribution fair.

### Disaster relief
- **Gummi flood relief** (Aug 2024; C_AhMUzMbXb): visited the Emir of Gummi; donated **15 trucks of emergency relief supplies** to thousands of affected families; set up a committee to rebuild damaged homes using "modern and durable materials". #ZamfaraFloodRelief
- Follow-up story (Sep 2026, @yarialheri; DdriFHahOaJ, DdR48GgpXuw): over **2,000 homes** were lost in Gummi; support came in stages — mattresses and blankets, then blocks, cement and money for builders, then roofing. Beneficiary featured: Hauwa'u Yushe'u.
- **Tudun Biri airstrike victims, Kaduna** (8 Dec 2023; C0m2_BPMz8P): visited with the Northern Senators Forum; **₦58 million donation** presented on behalf of the 58 Northern Senators.
