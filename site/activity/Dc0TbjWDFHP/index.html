Campaign 20273 September 2026 ZamfaraPosted with @everythingyari

Earlier this month, an appointment was made in Abuja.

Yesterday, the response came from the streets of Zamfara. Hundreds of thousands poured into Gusau in a remarkable show of support following Senator Abdul’aziz Yari’s appointment as Director-General of President Bola Ahmed Tinubu’s re-election campaign. It was a statement about Yari’s political capacity, grassroots strength, and the confidence that the people of his home state place in his ability to deliver. President Tinubu has given him the responsibility. The people have given him their support. Now, the work begins. @senatoraayari
