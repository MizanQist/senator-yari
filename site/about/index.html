About

Abdulaziz Abubakar Yari

Abdulaziz Abubakar Yari is the Senator representing Zamfara West in the 10th Senate, the former two-term Governor of Zamfara State, and, since August 2026, Director-General of the APC Presidential Campaign Council. He holds the traditional title Marafan Sokoto.

He was born and raised in Talata Mafara, Zamfara State, which he still calls home. His late father, Alhaji Abubakar Yari, remains his greatest inspiration: Baba's teachings, lessons and faith shaped the person he is today.

As Governor he welcomed Asiwaju Bola Ahmed Tinubu to Zamfara to commission township roads built by his administration, an early sign of a shared belief that infrastructure connects communities and drives growth.

In the Senate he chairs the Committee on Water Resources and sits on the joint committees on Water Resources and Sanitation and on Interior. He is a member of the Northern Senators Forum.

Outside the chamber his work in Zamfara runs through food distribution, disaster relief, scholarships, health outreach and infrastructure funded personally and through the constituency office.
