Senate work26 October 2023 National Assembly Complex Abuja

This afternoon, I presided over the inaugural meeting of the 10th Senate’s Committee on Water Resources.

At the meeting, I reiterated the Committee&#39;s commitment to working closely with the executive branch to ensure that all Nigerians have access to clean drinking water and adequate sanitation. We will also collaborate with relevant ministries, departments, and agencies to fully harness the potential of water for energy production, irrigation, and transportation.
