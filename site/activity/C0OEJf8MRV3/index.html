Projects & giving29 November 2023 Zamfara West

Recognizing the critical need for accessible eye care in Zamfara West Senatorial District, my constituency office, in collaboration with Visions Savers Eye Care Center, has launched a comprehensive eye care initiative, providing free eye treatments to 1,000 individuals in Zamfara West.

This initiative, designed to enhance the eye health of vulnerable groups, will provide my constituents with comprehensive treatment options, including surgeries, medications, and glasses, to restore and preserve their vision. I thank all our partners, particularly the medical professionals and our political associates, for their support in ensuring the impact of these treatments for those who need them most.
